# Design Context

- **Target audience:** A private investor managing a personal multi-account portfolio, primarily from an iPhone or Android phone.
- **Primary use cases:** Glance at total wealth and allocation, update holdings and prices, inspect positions, and review portfolio history with minimal friction.
- **Brand personality:** Calm, compact, and financially confident, with the product discipline and mobile ergonomics associated with Revolut. Preserve Worth's existing visual identity rather than redesigning it from scratch.
- **Platform context:** Touch-first installed PWA today, with a future path to native iOS and Android. Support Safari PWA lifecycle constraints, 320px widths, portrait and landscape, keyboard access, and desktop use.
